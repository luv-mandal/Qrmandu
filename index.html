How it works

From scan to Google review in 15 seconds

Customer scans your QR. Selects stars. Gets a natural review generated. One tap opens your direct Google review page. They paste and submit.

01
Business creates QR
Sign up, add your Google review link, upload logo, pick a QR design. Download and print.
02
Customer scans & rates
No app, no signup, no form. Customer just selects 1-5 stars. Review is generated automatically.
03
Submits on Google
Tap Submit Review. We open your exact Google review page and copy the review. Customer pastes and posts.
!
Honest handoff: We never fake Google submission. If browser restrictions prevent auto-inserting text into Google’s composer, we copy the review and show clear paste instructions. Final submission stays with the customer — exactly how Google wants it.

FAQ

Common questions

Do customers need to create an account?
No. Customer flow is locked: scan QR → select stars only → get generated review → copy → submit on Google. No name, email, phone, or manual writing.
What Google link do I need to provide?
Your direct Google Review Page Link (e.g., g.page/r/.../review or search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=...). We do not ask for a generic Maps search URL. We open that exact URL on Submit Review.
Can QRmandu automatically post to Google?
No, and we do not fake it. We open your direct review link, copy the generated review to clipboard, and show clear paste instructions. Final submission stays under customer control, as required by Google.
What happens after 24-hour trial?
Trial expires and you see three plans. Choose one, we open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Send payment proof, admin verifies, gives you a unique coupon like QRMD-X7K2-P9LA. Enter coupon to activate 1-month subscription.
Is the QR dynamic?
Yes. We never encode your Google URL directly. Printed QR is https://qrmandu.com/r/abc123 which routes through QRmandu to your review page. You can manage it later without reprinting.
Are generated reviews unique?
Yes. Every generation is unique, natural, simple-English, human-like, appropriate to the selected rating, non-repetitive, with varied opening, structure, length, vocabulary, rhythm, tone.
